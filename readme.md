## 📌 Project Overview

The **Barangay Tagapo Website** is the official portal of Barangay Tagapo, designed to:

- Provide convenient access to essential services  
- Enhance transparency in local governance  
- Promote community awareness and engagement  

Key areas include:

- 🏛️ Governance & barangay history  
- 🩺 Health & medical schedules  
- 🔒 Emergency & security services  
- 💼 Business permits & job postings  
- 👥 Youth council (SK) updates  
- 🌱 Environmental sustainability efforts  

---

## 🚀 Key Features by Section

### 🏠 Home Page

- **Historical Timeline** – Interactive history of Barangay Tagapo  
- **Barangay ID Guide** – Step-by-step with requirements  
- **Interactive Map** – Explore subdivisions via red map icons  
- **Featured Articles** – Community updates with “Read More”  
- **Officials Directory** – Leader profiles with navigation arrows  

💡 *Tip: Use arrow buttons to explore image galleries and click map markers for more details.*

---

### 🩺 Health Services

- **Medical Schedules** – Daily availability (Mon–Fri)  
- **Service Calendar** – View health activities like immunizations  
- **Free Services:**  
  - Tooth Extraction (Thursdays)  
  - TB DOTS  
  - Anti-Rabies Vaccination  
  - HIV Testing  

💡 *Tip: Click specific days on the calendar to view service requirements.*

---

### 🛡️ Security Services

- **Emergency Hotlines:**  
  - Barangay Hotline  
  - PNP Sta. Rosa  
  - CDRRMO  
  - Bureau of Fire Protection  

- **Tanod Map** – Visual outpost deployment  
- **Report Filing** – 3-step complaint procedure  
- **BPAT Profiles** – Peacekeeping team overview  

💡 *Tip: Hover over each step for more instructions or click “Contact Us” for immediate help.*

---

### 💼 Livelihood Programs

- **Business Permits:**  
  - Application guide  
  - 30+ categories  
  - Paginated tables  

- **Job Opportunities:**  
  - Employer listings  
  - Job details & benefits  
  - Pagination for browsing  

💡 *Tip: Use page buttons to explore all permits or click “Read More” for full job details.*

---

### 👥 Sangguniang Kabataan

- **Youth Officials** – Profiles with arrows  
- **Committee Structure** – Hover-reveal responsibilities  
- **Project Gallery** – Filtered events & initiatives  
- **SK History** – Evolution timeline  
- **Katipunan ng Kabataan** – Youth assembly info  

💡 *Tip: Browse projects using date filters and hover on cards for descriptions.*

---

### 🌿 Environment Initiatives

- **Programs:**  
  - Lamok Buster (mosquito control)  
  - Kaminero ni Etaw (cleanups)  
  - Adopt-A-Lot (green spaces)  

- **Impact Metrics** – Focused on 4 environmental pillars

- **Project Spotlights:**  
  - HAPAG (Urban Farming)  
  - Aquaponics Garden  
  - MRF  

- **Volunteer Portal** – Join via “Get Involved” buttons  

💡 *Tip: Click cards for program details. Use “Back to Top” for easier navigation.*
